Free parcel analysis and PDF report · Greek and English

01 / What you get

Everything mapped about a parcel, on one screen.

What you can build

Planning zone, buildable floor area, site coverage, floors and height from the official DLS zone parameters — per zone when a parcel has several.

Official checks

Geological suitability, seismic zone, landslides, floods, Natura 2000, coast protection, state land and more — each marked Check or Clear, with its source.

The parcel today

Structures on the DLS map and from satellite detection, the nearest mapped road, slope, elevation and land cover.

Near the parcel

The nearest pharmacy, school, shop, café, bus stop and park, with straight-line distances and every place on the map.

02 / How it works

Three steps.

  1. 01

    Find the parcel

    Search by address or place, by sheet / plan and parcel number as on the title deed, or click it on the map.

  2. 02

    Analyse

    TerrAX reads the official layers for that exact boundary — usually in under a minute.

  3. 03

    Read and share

    See the answers on screen and download the PDF report to share with a buyer, an architect or a partner.

03 / The report

A report you can share.

Every page carries its report number and a clear statement of what it is: a preliminary screening from official mapped data, not planning, legal or geotechnical advice.

  1. Summary: the parcel, the map, what you can build and what to check
  2. Planning and cadastre, zone by zone
  3. Risks and environment, with the body behind each check
  4. The parcel today and what is nearby
  5. Sources, dates and limitations

04 / Who it is for

Built for the people who decide on land.

Developers

Screen parcels quickly: what the zone allows, what is already there and which checks need a professional.

Real-estate professionals

Describe a property accurately and hand clients a clear, sourced report.

Owners and buyers

Understand your land before you sell, buy or brief an architect.

Built on data from the Department of Lands and Surveys, the Geological Survey Department, the Water Development Department, the Department of Environment, Copernicus and OpenStreetMap.
